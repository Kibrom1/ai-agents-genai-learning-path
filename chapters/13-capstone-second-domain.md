# Chapter 13: Capstone — Apply It to a Second Domain

*Phase 4 — Capstone Project*
## Build
Take the pattern from Chapter 12 and rebuild it against a second, different use case — ideally something from your own job or product. This is the strongest proof the pattern generalizes rather than being a one-off script, and it should reuse the same infrastructure (control plane, eval harness) as Chapter 12.

## Deliverable
A second end-to-end working agent, sharing infrastructure with the Chapter 12 capstone.
